1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In heavy rigging and industrial installation engineering, hydraulic gantry systems
(such as Lift Systems, Greiner, and Enerpac modular units) frequently operate near
both their mechanical hoist limits and the structural capacity of the host facility
floor slab. A routine point-load lifting operation can result in severe slab
cracking, lateral-torsional buckling of header beams, timber crushing, or lateral
instability if eccentricities, dynamic hoisting spikes, side-pull inertia, and
partial safety factors are neglected.

The Hydraulic Gantry & Track Bearing Load Analyzer (Rev 3.3) is a single-screen
static & ULS verification tool developed for preliminary lift planning under
Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and harmonized Eurocodes (EN 13001-2, EN 13001-3-1,
EN 1990, EN 1993-1-1, EN 1995-1-1). It computes:

  1. Split vertical reactions across dual gantry towers under variable hook
     configurations (Config 1: Single Hook Point Load; Config 2: Dual Hook 50/50 Split).
  2. Progressive deadweight accumulation across header lifting beams, side-shift/
     swivel assemblies, full hydraulic telescopic tower units (`unitWt`), gantry
     tracks, and calculated hardwood support arrays.
  3. Internal beam shear force V(x) and bending moment M(x) diagrams evaluated via
     Heaviside step functions across 60 equidistant intervals, plus ULS flexural &
     lateral-torsional buckling (LTB) resistance M_b,Rd (EN 1993-1-1 § 6.3.2) and
     stocked beam span overrun interlocking.
  4. Horizontal lateral / side-pull & travel acceleration stability (H_i = alpha_h * F_crown,i)
     producing base eccentricities, core exceedance checks (e <= B/6 vs overturning
     B/2), and amplified edge bearing stress (q_peak).
  5. Actual ground contact bearing pressure (q_actual in kN/m^2, selectable between
     Working/Characteristic q_adm and Eurocode ULS 1.35G + 1.50Q) based on the
     directional 45° longitudinal dispersion model and zero transverse dispersion (0°).
  6. Tier 3B steel-rail-to-hardwood perpendicular-to-grain crushing verification
     (sigma_c,90,Ed <= f_c,90,d) per EN 1995-1-1 § 6.1.5 across timber grades
     C24, D30, D40, and D70 when Hardwood block array mode is active.
  7. Synchronized out-of-level hydraulic tolerances (Delta H_span, Delta H_base) and
     an interactive Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC Annex I § 4.1.2.6 commissioning
     sign-off checklist.


2. STRUCTURAL MECHANICS & MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Applied Suspended Load, Dynamic Amplification & Partial Safety Factors
The total applied hook force incorporates the dynamic hoisting factor (phi) to
account for dynamic inertia, sling elongation, and hydraulic control pulsing:

$$P_{\text{dyn}} = P \cdot \phi \cdot \frac{g}{1000} \quad [\text{kN}]$$

Where:
  - $P$    = Gross suspended payload ($\text{kg}$)
  - $\phi$ = Hoisting / dynamic factor (dimensionless, $1.00\text{ to }1.25$
             pursuant to EN 13001-2 / DIN 15018).
             Values $\phi < 1.05$ trigger a live caution banner permitting
             $\phi = 1.00$ for static load test evaluation only.
  - $g$    = $9.80665\ \text{m/s}^2$ (standard gravity)

For Eurocode Ultimate Limit State (ULS) member checks (beam bending/LTB and
hardwood timber crushing, plus ULS slab mode when selected), permanent deadweight
components ($G$) receive $\gamma_G = 1.35$ and dynamic hoisted reactions ($Q$)
receive $\gamma_Q = 1.50$.


2.2 Beam-Level Statics & EN 1993-1-1 Bending / LTB Verification

Configuration 1: Single Hook Point Load
For an eccentric single hook located at distance $x$ from the center of Support A
along clear span $L$:

$$R_{\text{hook}, A} = P_{\text{dyn}} \cdot \left(\frac{L - x}{L}\right), \qquad R_{\text{hook}, B} = P_{\text{dyn}} \cdot \left(\frac{x}{L}\right)$$

Configuration 2: Dual Hook (Equal 50/50 Split)
When rigging with dual hoist blocks separated by $L_{\text{spread}}$, centered at
$x_{\text{center}}$ ($0 \le x_1 \le x_2 \le L$):

$$x_1 = x_{\text{center}} - \frac{L_{\text{spread}}}{2}, \qquad x_2 = x_{\text{center}} + \frac{L_{\text{spread}}}{2}$$
$$R_{\text{hook}, A} = \frac{P_{\text{dyn}}}{2} \cdot \left[\frac{(L - x_1) + (L - x_2)}{L}\right], \qquad R_{\text{hook}, B} = \frac{P_{\text{dyn}}}{2} \cdot \left[\frac{x_1 + x_2}{L}\right]$$

Header Lifting Beam Deadweight Distribution & Crown Reactions:
$$R_{\text{beam}, A} = R_{\text{beam}, B} = \frac{m_{\text{beam}} \cdot g}{2 \cdot 1000}, \qquad F_{\text{crown}, i} = R_{\text{hook}, i} + R_{\text{beam}, i} \quad [\text{kN}]$$

ULS Flexural & Lateral-Torsional Buckling (EN 1993-1-1 § 6.2.5 & § 6.3.2):
Using beam section properties ($W_{pl,y}, I_z, I_T, I_w, f_y = 355\ \text{MPa}$)
from `data/beams.js` and unbraced length $L_{cr} = L$:
  - Design moment $M_{\text{Ed}}$ is sampled across 60 intervals plus hook
    ordinates under $1.35\,w_{\text{beam}}$ and $1.50\,P_i$.
  - Plastic moment resistance: $M_{c,\text{Rd}} = W_{pl,y} \cdot f_y \cdot 10^{-3}\ [\text{kNm}]$ ($\gamma_{M0} = 1.0$).
  - Elastic critical moment ($C_1 = 1.0$ conservative):
    $$M_{cr} = \frac{\pi^2 E I_z}{L_{cr}^2} \sqrt{\frac{I_w}{I_z} + \frac{L_{cr}^2 G I_T}{\pi^2 E I_z}}$$
  - Slenderness $\bar{\lambda}_{LT} = \sqrt{M_{c,\text{Rd}} / M_{cr}}$; curve
    $b$ ($\alpha_{LT} = 0.34$) for $H/W \le 2.0$ or curve $c$ ($\alpha_{LT} = 0.49$)
    for $H/W > 2.0$, with $\lambda_{LT,0} = 0.4, \beta = 0.75$. Closed/twin-web
    frames (`309406`, `308496`) use $\chi_{LT} = 1.0$.
  - LTB resistance: $M_{b,\text{Rd}} = \chi_{LT} \cdot M_{c,\text{Rd}}$.
  - Stock Span Overrun Gate: For catalogued beams (`!isCustom`), if $L > L_{\text{stock}}$,
    a Tier 1 overrun banner fires and sets the overall gate to FAIL / OVERLOAD.


2.3 Internal Forces: Shear Force & Bending Moment Distributions
Evaluated piecewise across 60 equidistant intervals plus exact point-load
ordinates via Heaviside step function $\mathcal{H}(x - x_i)$:

$$V(x) = R_{\text{crown}, A} - (w_{\text{beam}} \cdot x) - \sum_{i} P_i \cdot \mathcal{H}(x - x_i)$$
$$M(x) = R_{\text{crown}, A} \cdot x - \frac{1}{2} w_{\text{beam}} \cdot x^2 - \sum_{i} P_i (x - x_i) \cdot \mathcal{H}(x - x_i)$$


2.4 Lateral Side-Pull / Skewing Stability & Synchronized Leveling (EN 13001-2 / 2006/42/EC)
Horizontal side-pull / skewing force per tower ($H_{\text{lat},i} = \alpha_h \cdot F_{\text{crown},i}$,
default $\alpha_h = 0.05$, selectable $0.00 / 0.05 / 0.10$) acts at cylinder pin
elevation $H_{\text{stage}}$:

$$M_{\text{lat},i} = H_{\text{lat},i} \cdot \frac{H_{\text{stage}}}{1000} \quad [\text{kNm}], \qquad e_i = \frac{M_{\text{lat},i} \cdot 1000}{F_{\text{base},i}} \quad [\text{mm}]$$
$$u_i = \max\left(\frac{e_i}{W_{\text{base}}}, \frac{e_i}{L_{\text{base}}}\right)$$

Edge contact magnification factor $k_{\text{edge},i}$:
  - Within middle-third core ($u_i \le 1/6$, i.e. $\le 33.3\%$ of $B/2$):
    $k_{\text{edge},i} = 1 + 6u_i$ ($1.00\text{ to }2.00$).
  - Partial base heel uplift ($1/6 < u_i < 0.5$): triggers amber stability alert
    with $k_{\text{edge},i} = \frac{4}{3(1 - 2u_i)}$.
  - Overturning ($u_i \ge 0.5$, i.e. $\ge 100\%$ of $B/2$): flags `UPLIFT` and fails gate.

Synchronized hydraulic leveling limit (`input_level_tol`, default $2.0\ \text{mm/m}$):
$$\Delta H_{\text{span}} = \pm L_{\text{m}} \cdot \text{tol}, \qquad \Delta H_{\text{base}} = \pm \frac{W_{\text{base}}}{1000} \cdot \text{tol} \quad [\text{mm}]$$


3. FLOOR LOADING, DISPERSION PHYSICS & HARDWOOD CRUSHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Cumulative Vertical Base Force
$$F_{\text{base}, i} = F_{\text{crown}, i} + W_{\text{shift}} + W_{\text{tracks\_tower}} + W_{\text{track\_unit}} \quad [\text{kN}]$$
(where $W_{\text{tracks\_tower}}$ from gantry `unitWt` already includes the complete
cylinder and top clevis assembly between the beam and tracks; $W_{\text{shift}}$ covers
optional top side-shift / swivel deadweight).

Slab Verification Basis (`input_q_basis`):
  - `working` (default): $F_{\text{eval},i} = F_{\text{base},i}$ checked against $q_{\text{allowable}}$.
  - `uls`: $F_{\text{eval},i} = 1.35\,G_{\text{base},i} + 1.50\,R_{\text{hook},i}$ checked against design slab limit $q_{\text{Rd}}$.


3.2 Ground Contact Area & Dual-Interface 45° Dispersion Geometry
Mode 1 — Direct Track-on-Slab (`Track on slab`):
  $L_{\text{eff}} = L_{\text{base}} + 2 \cdot h_{\text{track}}\ [\text{mm}]$, $W_{\text{eff}} = W_{\text{track}}\ [\text{mm}]$ ($0^\circ$ transverse).

Mode 2 — Hardwood Block Array (`Hardwood block array`):
  45° track cone is omitted; each block $[x_i, x_i + w_{\text{profile}}]$ disperses
  at $45^\circ$ only at the hardwood–concrete interface through $h_{\text{slab}}$
  ($I_i' = [x_i - h_{\text{slab}}, x_i + w_{\text{profile}} + h_{\text{slab}}]$, overlapping
  cones merged), with $W_{\text{eff}} = l_{\text{block}}$ ($l_{\text{block}} > W_{\text{track}}$ required).

$$A_{\text{eff}} = \frac{L_{\text{eff}} \cdot W_{\text{eff}}}{1\,000\,000}\ [\text{m}^2], \qquad q_i = \frac{F_{\text{eval},i}}{A_{\text{eff}}}, \qquad q_{\text{peak},i} = q_i \cdot k_{\text{edge},i} \quad [\text{kN/m}^2]$$


3.3 Tier 3B: Hardwood Crushing Perpendicular to Grain (EN 1995-1-1 § 6.1.5)
When `Hardwood block array` is active, contact stress directly beneath the steel
rail flange on top of the timber blocks is checked at ULS ($1.35G + 1.50Q$ above
the timber interface, i.e. excluding timber cribbing self-weight):

$$A_{\text{rail,timber}} = \frac{n \cdot w_{\text{profile}} \cdot W_{\text{track}}}{1\,000\,000} \quad [\text{m}^2]$$
$$F_{\text{timber,Ed},i} = 1.35\,(R_{\text{beam},i} + W_{\text{shift}} + W_{\text{tracks\_tower}} + W_{\text{rail}}) + 1.50\,R_{\text{hook},i} \quad [\text{kN}]$$
$$\sigma_{c,90,\text{Ed},i} = \frac{F_{\text{timber,Ed},i}}{A_{\text{rail,timber}}} \le f_{c,90,d} = \frac{k_{\text{mod}} \cdot f_{c,90,k}}{\gamma_M} \cdot 1000 \quad [\text{kN/m}^2]$$

Short-term rigging modification factors: $k_{\text{mod}} = 0.90$, $\gamma_M = 1.30$.
Timber self-weight per support is computed automatically from block volume and
EN 338 mean density ($\rho_{\text{mean}}$) as
$m_{\text{wood}} = n \cdot (l_{\text{block}} \cdot w_{\text{profile}} \cdot h_{\text{wood}} / 10^9) \cdot \rho_{\text{mean}}$:
  - `C24` ($f_{c,90,k} = 2.5\ \text{MPa}$, $\rho_{\text{mean}} = 420\ \text{kg/m}^3$) -> $f_{c,90,d} \approx 1{,}731\ \text{kN/m}^2$
  - `D30` ($f_{c,90,k} = 8.0\ \text{MPa}$, $\rho_{\text{mean}} = 640\ \text{kg/m}^3$) -> $f_{c,90,d} \approx 5{,}538\ \text{kN/m}^2$
  - `D40` ($f_{c,90,k} = 8.8\ \text{MPa}$, $\rho_{\text{mean}} = 700\ \text{kg/m}^3$, default) -> $f_{c,90,d} \approx 6{,}092\ \text{kN/m}^2$
  - `D70` ($f_{c,90,k} = 13.5\ \text{MPa}$, $\rho_{\text{mean}} = 900\ \text{kg/m}^3$) -> $f_{c,90,d} \approx 9{,}346\ \text{kN/m}^2$


4. MULTI-TIER STRUCTURAL VERIFICATION & GATING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Status bands: `PASS <= 85%`, `CAUTION 85–100%` (or $\phi < 1.05$ / heel core > 33%), `OVERLOAD / FAIL > 100%`:

  - Tier 1 (SWL + Span + EN 1993-1-1 LTB):
    1. $\max(F_{\text{crown}}) \le \text{SWL}_{\text{beam}}$ (or toggle $P_{\text{dyn}}$).
    2. Stock span check: $L \le L_{\text{stock}}$ (`!isCustom`).
    3. ULS Bending & LTB: $M_{\text{Ed}} \le M_{b,\text{Rd}}$.
  - Tier 2 (2-Pt Cylinder Rating + Lateral Stability + Sync Level):
    Option A checks $F_{\text{crown},i} \le \text{cap}_{2\text{pt}}$ (flagging
    missing ratings without guessing; 2-stage lock on `Lift Systems 70T`), plus
    EN 13001-2 horizontal side-pull eccentricity ($u_{\max} < 0.50$, edge peak
    monitoring) and 2006/42/EC sync out-of-level ($\Delta H_{\text{span}}, \Delta H_{\text{base}}$).
  - Tier 3 & 3B (Slab Bearing & Timber Crushing):
    Evaluates $\max(q_A, q_B) \le q_{\text{allowable}}$, outputs required mat area
    when exceeded, reports eccentric edge peak $q_{\text{peak}}$, and verifies
    EN 1995-1-1 hardwood crushing $\sigma_{c,90,\text{Ed}} \le f_{c,90,d}$.

6. ENGINEERING LIMITATIONS, BOUNDARY CONDITIONS & 2006/42/EC SIGN-OFF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Planar Mechanics + Side-Pull Approximation: Primary statics evaluate 2D planar
     equilibrium plus parameterized 1st-order lateral side-pull ($H_i = \alpha_h F_{\text{crown},i}$);
     extreme site wind loads, anchor tie-backs, and 2nd-order $P$-$\Delta$ effects
     require dedicated finite-element modeling.
  2. Track Span Continuity: Assumes continuous, level slab or inspected cribbing
     support without unbridged pits or expansion joints.
  3. Concrete Slab Punching & Subgrade Check: Evaluates average and edge-amplified
     contact pressure ($q, q_{\text{peak}}$); structural slab bending and punching
     shear ($v_{\text{Ed}}$) must still be verified against site civil drawings.
  4. Commissioning & Sign-Off (`2006/42/EC` Annex I § 4.1.2.6): Section 6 provides
     an interactive pre-lift commissioning checklist covering sync tolerance, rail
     anchorage/cribbing, header twist-lock/LTB restraint, and PE/Prüfingenieur approval.
