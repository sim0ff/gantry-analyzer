TECHNICAL WHITE PAPER & DESIGN METHODOLOGYDocument Reference: ENG-WP-HGA-001Project: Heavy Hydraulic Gantry & Track Bearing Load AnalyzerTarget Application: Heavy Industrial Rigging, Machine Installations, and Plant ShutdownsRevision: 3.0Date: October 20261. Executive Summary & ObjectiveIn heavy rigging and industrial installation engineering, hydraulic gantry systems (such as Lift Systems and Greiner modular units) frequently operate near both their mechanical hoist limits and the structural capacity of the host facility floor slab. A routine point-load lifting operation can result in severe slab cracking, punching failure, or tower buckling if eccentricities, dynamic hoisting spikes, and deadweight distributions are neglected.The Hydraulic Gantry & Track Load Analyzer is a single-screen static analysis and verification tool developed to eliminate manual calculation errors during preliminary lift planning. It computes:Split vertical reactions across dual gantry towers under variable hook configurations.Progressive deadweight accumulation across lifting beams, crown headers, hydraulic telescopic legs, and runway rail tracks.Actual ground contact bearing pressure ($q_{\text{actual}}$ in $\text{kN/m}^2$) based on a directional $45^\circ$ longitudinal dispersion model.Three-tier utilization gating: Lifting Beam Capacity, Gantry Stage Capacity (Option A), and Allowable Floor Limits.2. Structural Mechanics & Mathematical Formulation2.1 Applied Suspended Load & Dynamic AmplificationThe total applied hook force incorporates the dynamic hoisting factor ($\phi$) to account for dynamic inertia, sling elongation, and hydraulic control pulsing:$$P_{\text{dyn}} = P \cdot \phi \cdot \frac{g}{1000} \quad [\text{kN}]$$Where:$P$ = Gross suspended payload ($\text{kg}$)$\phi$ = Hoisting / dynamic factor (dimensionless, typically $1.05\text{ to }1.20$ pursuant to EN 13001-2 / DIN 15018)$g = 9.80665\ \text{m/s}^2$2.2 Beam-Level Statics (Reactions at Crown Supports)Configuration 1: Single Hook Point LoadFor an eccentric single hook located at distance $x$ from the center of Support A along clear span $L$:$$R_{\text{hook}, A} = P_{\text{dyn}} \cdot \left(\frac{L - x}{L}\right)$$$$R_{\text{hook}, B} = P_{\text{dyn}} \cdot \left(\frac{x}{L}\right)$$Configuration 2: Dual Hook (Equal 50/50 Split)When rigging with a spreader frame or dual hoist blocks separated by distance $L_{\text{spread}}$, centered at $x_{\text{center}}$:$$x_1 = x_{\text{center}} - \frac{L_{\text{spread}}}{2}, \quad x_2 = x_{\text{center}} + \frac{L_{\text{spread}}}{2}$$$$P_1 = P_2 = \frac{P_{\text{dyn}}}{2}$$$$R_{\text{hook}, A} = \frac{P_{\text{dyn}}}{2} \cdot \left[\frac{(L - x_1) + (L - x_2)}{L}\right]$$$$R_{\text{hook}, B} = \frac{P_{\text{dyn}}}{2} \cdot \left[\frac{x_1 + x_2}{L}\right]$$Header Lifting Beam Deadweight DistributionThe self-weight of the lifting beam ($W_{\text{beam}}$ in $\text{kN}$) is treated as an ideal uniformly distributed load ($w_{\text{beam}} = \frac{W_{\text{beam}}}{L}$), distributing equally to each crown header:$$R_{\text{beam}, A} = R_{\text{beam}, B} = \frac{W_{\text{beam}}}{2} = \frac{m_{\text{beam}} \cdot g}{2 \cdot 1000}$$Total Crown Reaction Force ($F_{\text{crown}}$)The total mechanical reaction applied directly to the top of each hydraulic gantry tower is:$$F_{\text{crown}, A} = R_{\text{hook}, A} + R_{\text{beam}, A} \quad [\text{kN}]$$$$F_{\text{crown}, B} = R_{\text{hook}, B} + R_{\text{beam}, B} \quad [\text{kN}]$$2.3 Internal Forces: Shear Force & Bending Moment DistributionsThe lifting beam internal shear force $V(x)$ and bending moment $M(x)$ along the longitudinal axis ($0 \le x \le L$) are evaluated using piecewise equilibrium equations:$$V(x) = R_{\text{crown}, A} - (w_{\text{beam}} \cdot x) - \sum_{i} P_i \cdot \mathcal{H}(x - x_i)$$$$M(x) = R_{\text{crown}, A} \cdot x - \frac{1}{2} w_{\text{beam}} \cdot x^2 - \sum_{i} P_i (x - x_i) \cdot \mathcal{H}(x - x_i)$$Where $\mathcal{H}(x - x_i)$ is the Heaviside step function:$$\mathcal{H}(x - x_i) = \begin{cases} 0 & x < x_i \\ 1 & x \ge x_i \end{cases}$$The analyzer samples these equations at 60 equidistant discrete intervals across the span to render the Shear Force Diagram (SFD) and Bending Moment Diagram (BMD).3. Floor Loading & Longitudinal $45^\circ$ Dispersion Physics3.1 Cumulative Vertical Base ForceThe total vertical force exerted by each gantry assembly onto its supporting track and foundation accumulates deadweights that do not stress the header beam:$$F_{\text{base}, i} = F_{\text{crown}, i} + W_{\text{crown\_dead}} + W_{\text{tracks\_tower}} + W_{\text{runway\_unit}} \quad [\text{kN}]$$Where:$W_{\text{crown\_dead}}$ = Mass of hydraulic crown, side-shift cylinders, and top swivel bearings ($\text{kN}$).$W_{\text{tracks\_tower}}$ = Mass of telescopic stages, main cylinder barrels, propulsion drives, and chassis shoe ($\text{kN}$).$W_{\text{runway\_unit}}$ = Self-weight of the runway rail section under the gantry footprint ($\text{kN}$).3.2 Ground Contact Area & Dispersion GeometryA critical flaw in generic lift calculators is assuming that the ground pressure distributes along the entire length of the rail ($2\text{--}6\text{ m}$). In reality, steel tracks behave elastically, transferring force through their web and bottom flange at a load dispersion angle before reaching the sub-base or concrete slab. Longitudinal Dispersion ($L_{\text{eff}}$)Assuming a classic $45^\circ$ ($1:1$) stress propagation through the structural depth of the rail profile ($h_{\text{track}}$):$$L_{\text{eff}} = L_{\text{base}} + 2 \cdot h_{\text{track}} \quad [\text{mm}]$$Transverse Dimension ($W_{\text{eff}}$) — The "Zero Dispersion" RuleIn standard hydraulic gantry track setups, the gantry leg base shoe width matches the track flange width ($W_{\text{base}} \approx W_{\text{track}}$). Because there is no structural steel overhang in the transverse direction:$$W_{\text{eff}} = W_{\text{track}} \quad [\text{mm}]$$Design Rule: Transverse dispersion is set to zero. Assuming lateral dispersion overestimates contact area and unsafely underreports floor pressure.Contact Bearing Area ($A_{\text{eff}}$) and Pressure ($q$)$$A_{\text{eff}} = \frac{L_{\text{eff}} \cdot W_{\text{eff}}}{1\,000\,000} \quad [\text{m}^2]$$$$q_A = \frac{F_{\text{base}, A}}{A_{\text{eff}}} \quad \left[\frac{\text{kN}}{\text{m}^2}\right], \qquad q_B = \frac{F_{\text{base}, B}}{A_{\text{eff}}} \quad \left[\frac{\text{kN}}{\text{m}^2}\right]$$4. Multi-Tier Structural Verification & GatingThe software performs three concurrent structural checks, flagging any violation with real-time UI banners and color-coded status warnings:Tier 1: Lifting Beam Safe Capacity CeilingCheck: $\max(F_{\text{crown}, A}, F_{\text{crown}, B}) \le \text{Capacity}_{\text{beam\_rated}}$Criteria: Reaction forces at beam ends must not exceed the pre-approved manufacturer Safe Working Load (SWL) for the selected profile.Note: This check is treated as a hard ceiling. Rigging engineers must verify unbraced compression flange lengths against lateral torsional buckling (LTB) when using long spans.Tier 2: Gantry Cylinder Extension Check (Option A vs. Option B)Option A (Active by Default): Evaluates $F_{\text{crown}}$ against the manufacturer's 2-point lift rating for the selected cylinder stage (Retracted, Stage 1, Stage 2, or Stage 3).If a unit has no published 2-point rating (e.g., Greiner 620XL T or Lift Systems 620T II in certain multi-stage configurations), the software marks it as NO PUBLISHED 2-PT RATING and does not assume an arbitrary 50% split.Option B: Bypasses cylinder stage verification for preliminary equilibrium framing or when 4-point coupled sync lifts are operated via independent software.Tier 3: Floor & Slab Bearing Stress CheckCheck: $\max(q_A, q_B) \le q_{\text{allowable}}$Criteria: Contact pressure must remain below the engineer-verified slab capacity (typically $5\text{ to }30\ \text{kN/m}^2$ for industrial plant slabs). If $q > q_{\text{allowable}}$, load-spreading mats (hardwood crane mats or composite steel plates) are required.5. Software Architecture & Implementation DetailsRuntime Environment: Single-file standalone HTML5, vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), and CSS3 (Tailwind CSS engine via CDN).Dependencies: Zero backend services; diagrams powered by SVG and Chart.js.Hosting Interoperability: Compatible with GitHub Pages, Azure Static Web Apps, and Microsoft Teams Tab integrations.Input Standards:Dimensions: Integer millimeters ($\text{mm}$).Masses: Integer kilograms ($\text{kg}$).Soil/Floor Pressures: $\text{kN/m}^2$.6. Engineering Limitations & Boundary ConditionsUsers must recognize the following boundary constraints when utilizing this analyzer:Quasi-Static Model: The analyzer models 2D planar equilibrium. It does not compute 3D lateral crabbing, track misalignments, or wind forces.Track Span Continuity: Calculations assume track sections are continuously supported by a uniform, flat slab. If tracks span across floor pits, drainage trenches, or expansion joints, beams must be engineered as unsupported bridge spans.Rigid Base Assumption: Ground pressure is calculated as an average equivalent contact stress ($q = F/A$). Edge concentrations due to base tilting or soft soil elasticity are not resolved.Sign-off Requirement: This tool serves as a planning aid and does not supersede signed and stamped lift studies executed by a licensed Professional Engineer (PE / Prüfingenieur).End of Technical Specification.
